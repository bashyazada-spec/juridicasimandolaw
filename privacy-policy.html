Privacy Policy

Simando Law Case Manager

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide our case management services, including client names, contact details, and case-related documents. All data is stored securely in Firebase and Google Drive under your control.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for managing legal cases and client relationships. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

Client data is stored in Google Firebase. Documents are stored in your own Google Drive account. We do not maintain separate copies of your files.

4. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your data at any time by removing profiles and cases from the application.

5. Contact

For privacy concerns, contact your system administrator.
