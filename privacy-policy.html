Privacy Policy

Simando Law Case Management System · Effective 2026

Summary: Simando Law collects only the data required for legal case management. Case narratives and docket metadata are encrypted client-side with AES-GCM 256. We never sell or share client or firm data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Details: Name, designation, contact number, optional profile photo, bio description, and email address.
  • Case Records: Case titles, docket numbers, filing dates, venues, involved parties, hearings, deadlines, and narratives.
  • Documents & Drafts: Uploaded PDFs, Word files, and linked Google Docs.
  • Communications: Online presence status, firm chat messages, and schedule proposals.

2. Google OAuth Usage

  • Google Drive & Docs (drive.file): Used strictly to store case attachments, profile photos, and drafted legal Google Docs inside your authorized Drive folders.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.events, calendar.readonly): Used strictly to sync case hearings and deadlines.

3. Security & Confidentiality

Sensitive case details are encrypted with AES-GCM 256 prior to cloud storage, protected by Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) and TLS transport security. Access is limited to the owning attorney, explicitly shared associates, and authorized administrators.

4. Data Rights & Retention

Aligned with the Philippine Data Privacy Act (RA 10173), you retain full ownership of all entered data and may edit records, disconnect Google access in Settings, or request account deletion at any time.

5. Contact

Questions or requests may be directed to admin@simandolaw.com.
