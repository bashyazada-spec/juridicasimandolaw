Privacy Policy

Simando Law — Case Management System

Summary: Simando Law respects attorney and client confidentiality. We collect only the information required to operate secure case tracking and document management, and we never sell or share firm data with third parties.

1. Information We Collect

To operate the Case Management System, we process the following categories of information:

  • Account Credentials: Full name, professional title, contact number, practice description, and authorized email address.
  • Case Records: Case titles, docket numbers, filing dates, court venues, parties involved, hearings, deadlines, and case narratives.
  • Attached Documents: Legal filings and Google Docs drafts stored via your linked Google Drive account.
  • Google Integration Data: OAuth access tokens and folder references when you authorize Google Drive, Docs, and Calendar.

2. Google User Data & OAuth Scopes

When you connect your Google Account, Simando Law requests only least-privilege scopes:

  • Google Drive & Docs (drive.file): Used strictly to create firm case folders, upload attachments, draft pleadings in Google Docs, and store profile photos. We only access files created or opened by this application.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.events & calendar.readonly): Used strictly to display upcoming hearings on your dashboard and synchronize case deadlines.

3. Encryption, Storage & Security

Sensitive case fields are encrypted client-side using AES-GCM 256 before being stored in Google Firebase Firestore. Uploaded files live directly inside your authorized Google Drive folders. Two-Factor Authentication (TOTP) and strict role-based rules protect account access.

4. Confidentiality & Data Sharing

Consistent with legal ethics and attorney-client privilege, case files are visible only to the owning attorney and associates explicitly granted access via case sharing or accepted appearance proposals.

5. Retention & Contact

You retain full ownership of all data entered into Simando Law. For privacy inquiries or deletion requests, contact admin@simandolaw.com.
